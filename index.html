FAQ

How do I add this to Notion?

Copy your embed link, go to Notion, type /embed, paste the link, and confirm.

Can I change the theme later?

Yes. Come back to this builder page, update your settings, and copy the new link.

Why is my widget background showing?

Use the background option to switch between light, dark, or system mode.
